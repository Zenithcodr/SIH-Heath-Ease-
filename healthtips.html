Daily Health Tips

1. Drink enough water and avoid long dehydration periods.

2. Sleep for 7-8 hours with a regular schedule.

3. Include protein, vegetables, and fruit in every major meal.

4. Do at least 30 minutes of physical activity.

Warning Signs

Seek immediate care for severe chest pain, breathing trouble, stroke signs, or heavy bleeding.
